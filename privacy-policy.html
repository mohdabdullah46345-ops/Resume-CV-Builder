Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

At ResumeForge ("we," "us," or "our"), your privacy is a top priority. This Privacy Policy explains what information we handle, how it is used, and the choices available to you when you use our website and resume builder (the "Service").

1. Information you enter into the builder

The resume builder lets you enter personal details such as your name, contact information, work history, and education. This information is stored only in your own web browser using local storage. It is not transmitted to or stored on our servers. If you clear your browser data or use a different device, this information will not be available.

2. Information we collect automatically

Like most websites, we and our service providers may collect limited technical information automatically, such as your browser type, device type, approximate location (derived from IP address), and pages visited. This helps us understand how the Service is used and improve it.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, measure traffic, and serve advertising. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not function as intended without them.

4. Advertising and Google AdSense

We may display advertisements through third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense. These partners may use cookies and device identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to our website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

5. How we use information

We use the limited technical information we collect to operate, maintain, and improve the Service, analyze usage trends, ensure security, and display relevant advertising that supports keeping the Service free.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited technical and usage data with trusted service providers (such as analytics and advertising partners) who help us operate the Service, and we may disclose information if required by law.

7. Data security

Because the personal details you enter into the builder remain in your browser, you retain direct control over that data. We take reasonable measures to protect the website itself, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, and to object to certain processing. Since builder data is stored locally, you can remove it at any time by clearing your browser storage or using the "Reset" button in the builder.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our Contact page.
