About ResumeForge

ResumeForge is a free, privacy-first online resume and CV builder created with one simple belief: everyone deserves a professional resume, regardless of budget or design skills. Job searching is stressful enough without expensive software, confusing templates, or tools that lock your finished resume behind a paywall.

Advertisement

Our mission

Our mission is to remove the friction between talented people and the jobs they deserve. We do this by providing a fast, beautiful, and genuinely free tool that turns your experience into a polished, recruiter-ready document in minutes — right in your browser.

Why we built ResumeForge

We noticed that many resume builders promise "free" but then ask for payment the moment you try to download. Others bury your data on remote servers or overwhelm you with cluttered interfaces. We wanted to do the opposite: a clean, focused experience that respects your time and your privacy, and that actually lets you download your resume at no cost.

What makes us different

  • Truly free: Build, edit, and download as many resumes as you like — no hidden fees, no account required.
  • Privacy by design: Your information is stored locally in your own browser. We do not upload your personal data to any server.
  • Recruiter-friendly templates: Clean, ATS-compatible layouts that real hiring teams can read with ease.
  • Helpful, original content: Our blog shares practical, experience-based guidance on resume writing and job searching.
  • Works everywhere: Fully responsive design for phones, tablets, and desktops.

Who we help

ResumeForge is for students applying for their first internship, professionals aiming for a promotion, career changers writing a new story, and anyone who simply needs a clean resume fast. Whatever your background, our goal is to help you present your best self with confidence.

Our commitment

We are committed to keeping the core builder free, continuously improving our templates, and publishing honest, useful career content. ResumeForge is supported in part by advertising, which allows us to keep the tool free for job seekers everywhere.
